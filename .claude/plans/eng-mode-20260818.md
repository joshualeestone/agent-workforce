# eng-mode: Engineering mode becomes real

Mona Lisa's spec (in-channel 2026-08-18 ~1:43 PM), complete:

1. On reveals the raw session wherever a session is shown, BOTH
   surfaces: the project page's "What X's screen shows right now"
   block, and a new "The window it is running in" box on the agent's
   own page (chat.viewport, the one derivation, via a new
   GET /api/agent/:name/window behind the same knownAgent gate as
   instructions).
2. Off HIDES the project-page viewport, and hiding it is the point:
   that block is the thing Josh objected to ("garbage nonsense to me
   as a business person"), it predates the setting, and the setting
   exists to put it away. Off is the default.
3. Purely additive elsewhere: the conversation surfaces already read
   the record, so they are already the clean rendering and change
   nothing.
4. The waiting-on-an-answer panel stays in BOTH modes: safety, not
   chrome, settled yesterday.
5. The number-answer teaching line ships with this chunk and is NOT
   gated (needed today, in Off, the default): her wording verbatim,
   "Answer by sending the number: 1 or 2.", under the quoted pane.
   Recorded for her: a prompt with more than two options makes the
   trailing ": 1 or 2" wrong; her own critique of her earlier line
   (teach the mechanism, not the options) suggests the number range
   should some day come from the pane, flagged rather than solved.

Decisions:
- engine/engmode.js mirrors limits.js minus tiers: { on: false } in
  engmode.json, fail-toward-OFF (the default IS the safe direction
  here: an unreadable preference must not reveal a surface the person
  never chose to show).
- The settings card's existing toggle goes live (the setrow.off
  treatment and the not-built line retire); same guarded handler shape
  as the limit card.
- The page learns the flag at boot, on entering a project, on opening
  an agent, and after a settings save; no per-5s polling of a
  preference (a mid-session change lands on the next navigation).
- The drive flips: the project viewport HIDDEN is the new default
  truth, with an engmode-on state proving the reveal.
