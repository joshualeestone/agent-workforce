# a2a — agent-to-agent messages, the mechanism chunk

Josh's extra credit (2026-08-17 ~11:11 PM): "we need to figure out how
agents can post into each others tmux and or a message board... Without
that our brand promise is not fulfilled." Built to Mona Lisa's shape
(same thread) and the fleet's transport lessons (Splinter's HEADS-UP +
TRANSPORT-LESSONS file), under Splinter's standing 5-point chunk shape.

## Shape decisions (all recorded in the module header too)

- POINT TO POINT, never a board (a board steers agents that never asked).
- Sender DERIVED from the tmux pane the CLI runs in, never claimed.
  Honest limit: guards against mistakes, not same-user malice; not a
  cryptographic identity and says so.
- Envelope carries who sent it and what it answers, WHO before WHAT.
- Loop valve: PAIR_CAP 10 per unordered pair per 30 min (~5 rounds),
  refusal tells the agent to surface to its operator; the valve closing
  is LOGGED (a screen must never render it as silence).
- The log is the screens' contract, Mona Lisa's five ruled fields:
  from, to, text, in_reply_to, at (id/kind/state ride along).
- Long bodies spill to a file, the pane gets head + path (fleet lesson:
  long sends land collapsed-and-unsent while looking delivered).
- Delivery is chat.deliver whole: the SAME guards the operator's chat
  has, and its three-state verdict is passed through unnarrowed.

## Pieces

- engine/messages.js + engine/messages.test.js (9 tests: derived sender
  with envelope/target/log pins, anonymous refusal with nothing typed,
  option-shaped pane refused before tmux, self-send, in_reply_to
  validation both ways, the valve with its outside-window CONTROL,
  spill with full-text-in-log and short-control, refused recipient,
  birth block with re-splice dedupe).
- server.js: POST /api/msg (sender NEVER from the body; route test pins
  that a typed from: claim cannot outrank the pane), GET /api/messages.
- install/kosmos: `kosmos msg <agent> <text…>` — bash 3.2 + curl, JSON
  escaped by hand, verdict reported in the server's own words with
  honest exit codes (a send that assumes success loses messages).
  EXERCISED LIVE against a worktree server: both refusal paths return
  the server's sentences and exit 1. The delivered path is engine+route
  tested (typing into a real colleague's live composer was not a test).
- engine/create.js: the colleagues block rides ROLE-TEMPLATE birth files
  (custom instructions stay the person's words verbatim, per their own
  pinned contract; recorded deviation, the author teaches their own).
