Your task board

      

        Kosmos gives you a simple task list to start with, and it is the one
        on your projects. Later you will be able to connect a board you
        already use instead of moving your work into ours.

        Notion
        Asana
        monday.com
        ClickUp
        Linear
        Airtable
      

      
None of these are connected yet. Trello is the one being built first.

      

        Kosmos is open source. If you want a different one before we get to
        it, you can wire it up yourself.
      

      

        Your tasks stay where they are. Kosmos reads them and does not copy
        them, so nothing here can disagree with your board.
