Kosmos Setup

Step 1 of 6

Welcome

This is where you see the agents working for you, and where you make new ones. All of it runs on this computer.

There is no account to make and no email to give. Nothing here signs you up for anything.

The record of everything your agents do is written down and kept on this Mac, in full. It is yours and it stays here.

Your agents think with Claude, so what you ask them goes to Claude to be answered. Nothing else about you is sent anywhere, and there is no account here to send it to.

Next: a quick look at this computer, then Claude, then your agents, then how you get back to Kosmos later.
